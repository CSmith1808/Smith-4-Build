What are they?

Transformers and Gundams might look similar at first glance, but they come from two different science-fiction universes.

Gundam

Gundams are giant humanoid machines called Mobile Suits. They are operated by human pilots and are used as advanced weapons of war.

Transformers

Transformers are sentient robotic beings from the planet Cybertron. They are living mechanical organisms capable of transforming into different objects, usually vehicles.

What's the difference?

Transformers can think, communicate, make decisions and develop relationships. They are divided into two main factions:

  • Autobots: the heroes who fight for freedom and peace.
  • Decepticons: the villains who seek power and control

Gundams are controlled by human pilots and its actions depend on the the people operating them. Their primary purpose is to serve as weapons in military combat. A few famous Gundam models include:

  • RX-78-2 Gundam
  • Wing Gundam Zero
  • Strike Noir Gundam

Popularity

Themes
